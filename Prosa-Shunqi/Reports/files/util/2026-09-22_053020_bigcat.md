The source audit confirmed three distinct ordered-list aggregation interfaces; they must not be conflated with finite sets:

- natural interval concatenation over the half-open interval `[m,n)`;
- ordinal concatenation over all values of `'I_n` in ordinal order;
- filtered/unfiltered concatenation over an input sequence, preserving its order and multiplicity.

`bigcat_partitions` is especially important: its source conclusion is equality of Boolean membership observations. It is not merely an arbitrary propositional membership equivalence.

Migration evidence is mixed. Three early lemmas have high-confidence historical candidates that require v0.6 adaptation, `mem_bigcat_ord` has only unverified Classic reference material, and the remaining declarations are v0.6 additions. The old file is therefore proof-strategy reference only; it is not copied wholesale.

Planned correspondence reuse: certified Nat/order/equality, eqType/DecidableEq, ordered seq/List, membership, append, filter, length, uniqueness/Nodup, and interval-sum bridges. New work is limited to the actual big-concatenation operation interfaces and their compositions.

- `size_big_nat` deliberately targets `Finset.Ico` for the sum value, matching the approved interval policy, while the concatenation itself remains list-based.
- `bigcat_partitions` retains equality of two decided Boolean membership observations.
