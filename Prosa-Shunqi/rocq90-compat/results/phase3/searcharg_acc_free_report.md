Date: 2026-09-22 (Asia/Hong_Kong)

Final classification: **`ACC_FREE_PATH_PARTIAL`**

## Scope

This phase tests only the compiled Lean declaration
`Prosa.Util.SearchArg.search_arg` and its two compiler-generated computation
equations. It does not change the SearchArg translation, Lean/Mathlib versions,
Rocq kernel, Phase 2 baseline/candidate, other pipelines, or formal acceptance
state. Phase 2 reports and logs remain unchanged.

`d12a710cef8f9d4d796b93de9e54adb6472a890c`. The Rocq 9.0 environment and the
Phase 2 stock importer were reused.

## Minimal export boundary

The selected roots are exactly:

1. `Prosa.Util.SearchArg.search_arg`;
2. `Prosa.Util.SearchArg.search_arg.eq_1`;
3. `Prosa.Util.SearchArg.search_arg.eq_2`.

All reachable theorem proof bodies are exported. No declaration is emitted as
`#AX`, no definition body is replaced, and no normalization/projection is
enabled in the final artifact. The resulting closure is 69,964 bytes and 99
imported entries, compared with 191 entries in the Phase 2 SearchArg export.

The boundary contains no name matching `Acc`, `WellFounded`, `Acc_rec`, or
`Acc_rect`. It retains the small `Nat.brecOn`/`Nat.below` implementation closure
generated by Lean's equation compiler. An audited `Meta.reduceAll` experiment
did not eliminate that closure and made the serialized artifact larger. It was
therefore not used. Removing the residual recursion machinery would require a
new body projection or representation argument not established in this phase.

As a negative comparison, exporting every SearchArg theorem with every proof
body produced a 2,208,305-byte closure containing `Acc`, `WellFounded`, and
three Lean axiom records. That closure is not the Phase 3 artifact.

## Stock Rocq 9.0 import

The proof-complete Acc-free artifact imports successfully with Rocq 9.0.0 and
the Phase 2 **baseline** importer. The baseline source has
`with_unsafe_univs f () = f ()`; it contains no
`check_universes=false` path. The Phase 2 unsafe candidate is neither loaded
nor applied.

The import produced 99 entries, 171 possible instances, 18 universe
expressions, and no non-syntactic inductive arities. A post-import explicit
universe inconsistency is rejected, providing a negative check that normal
universe checking remains active. Attempts to resolve imported `Acc`,
`WellFounded`, `Acc_rec`, and `Acc_rect` also fail, as intended.
